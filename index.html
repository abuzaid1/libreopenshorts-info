LibreOpenShorts is a privately operated application for creating and publishing short videos. The operator creates video clips locally, chooses the destination platforms and submits them to a publishing queue.

How YouTube access is used

The YouTube integration asks for permission to upload videos to the channel you authorize. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the video and the title, description and privacy setting selected in the local application. You authorize access through Google; the application does not ask for your Google password.

Local operation

The application and publishing queue run on the operator's device. This website contains only information and policies. It does not host the editor, collect Google credentials or offer an online sign-in service.

Access to YouTube is optional. The operator can revoke it through their Google Account. Review our privacy policy before authorizing access.

Support

For this private installation, contact the operator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
