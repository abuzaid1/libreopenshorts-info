Privacy policy

This policy describes the privately operated LibreOpenShorts application used for video publishing. The operator controls the device, connected accounts and stored information.

Google and YouTube information

When you authorize YouTube uploads, Google supplies OAuth tokens that allow the application to act within the permissions you grant. The application sends the selected video, title, description, tags and privacy setting to YouTube. It stores publishing results such as video IDs, queue status and error information locally.

The upload integration requests the YouTube upload permission. It does not use this permission to read Gmail, contacts or unrelated Google services.

Purpose and data use

Information received from Google is used to perform the publishing functions you request and diagnose publishing failures. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. LibreOpenShorts's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials, the publishing queue, media and delivery records are stored on the operator's device. The operator must restrict device access, keep credentials out of public repositories and protect backups. Tokens are transmitted to Google's OAuth and YouTube API endpoints to refresh authorization and upload content.

Other publishing destinations

Sending content to another platform requires selecting that destination in the application. An Instagram upload can use an expiring video link through Cloudflare so Meta can fetch the selected clip. That route does not send Google OAuth credentials to Meta or Cloudflare. Review the selected providers' policies for their handling of transmitted content.

Retention and deletion

Local media, queue history and configuration remain until the operator deletes them. To remove local Google authorization, remove the saved YouTube credentials and restart the publishing integration. Manage or delete already uploaded videos through YouTube; deleting local files does not delete them from YouTube.

Revoke access

You can revoke this app's access in your Google Account connections settings. Revocation prevents future authorized requests but does not automatically delete previously uploaded videos or local publishing records.

This informational website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, login forms or credential collection. The website host may process connection information under its own policies.

Contact and changes

Contact the installation operator using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen. Material changes to these practices should be reflected here before the changed behavior is used.
